THE CHALLENGE

How do you launch a tracksuit collection while celebrating individuality rather than just the product?

THE IDEA

It's not what you wear. It's how you make it yours.

THE EXECUTION

We turned the collection into a platform for self-expression. Starting with a silhouette-led teaser, we invited different stylists to reinterpret the tracksuits through their own personal style. Raw, street-inspired photography, direct flash and cool tones brought authenticity to the campaign, connecting every piece through one message: Own it. Make it yours.
